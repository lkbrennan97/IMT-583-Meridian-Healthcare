\section{Detailed Scope Statement}\label{sec:1}
\subsection{Project scope description}\label{sec:1.1}
Meridian Health currently manages training for approximately 40,000 employees across 12 hospitals and 30 outpatient clinics through scattered paper records and instructor-led sessions. This project will purchase and configure a centralized Learning Management System (LMS) to deliver training suitable for online completion, maintain employee training and certification records, and support compliance reporting.


% Source GP4 Skeleton block 11
The project will configure employee accounts with Meridian single sign-on (SSO), role- and department-based assignments, completion tracking and standard reporting on Meridian's internal network. It will convert existing HR-approved PDF, DOCX, PPTX and Markdown materials and migrate validated training histories and certification records, including verified external certifications.


% Source GP4 Skeleton block 12
Content conversion is limited to existing HR-approved training materials and existing packaged courses. The LMS will support vendor-compatible content formats, including SCORM 1.2 or xAPI where applicable to existing packaged courses. Existing instructional activities, quiz logic and media assets will be preserved where supported by the selected platform. Creating new curriculum, instructional videos, quizzes or interactive learning materials is excluded. Any additional content development or packaging requirements must be evaluated through the formal change request process under Section 1.11.


% Source GP4 Skeleton block 13
Employees receive onboarding materials and support. IT receives the operating runbooks needed for handover. The CHRO sponsors the project. HR and Finance are the pilot departments, with representative clinical-role and certification scenarios reviewed by trainers and Compliance. Ongoing automated HRIS synchronization is excluded.


% Source GP4 Skeleton block 14
Delivery will include requirements confirmation, vendor selection, platform configuration, content and record migration, pilot testing, and phased deployment across Meridian. The project will follow a primarily waterfall approach, with sequential phases and formal approvals before progressing to the next phase. Within configuration and pilot testing, feedback from HR, IT, Compliance, trainers, and pilot users will guide revisions and retesting before approval.


% Source GP4 Skeleton block 15
The project is complete when the approved LMS configuration is deployed across Meridian, in-scope content and records meet the acceptance criteria, user onboarding and final-wave stabilization are completed, critical defects are resolved, and IT/\allowbreak{}LMS administrators and HR record owners accept their respective operational responsibilities. The working closure forecast is January 6, 2028. December 31, 2027 remains the original completion target for variance reporting. Full rollout for benefits measurement means completion of the final deployment wave on December 9, 2027. The target is a 60--65\% reduction in monthly HR administration hours by June 9, 2028. The HR Records Lead maintains the operational logs and the PM assesses the benefit using the baseline and method in Section 6.3. This post-rollout assessment is separate from project closure.

\subsection{Features and functionality}\label{sec:1.3}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Employee accounts and access:} Employees sign in through Meridian SSO on the internal network. All system administration, identity configuration, privileged access approval and record-maintenance permissions are owned by Meridian IT, including the IT-based LMS Administrator. HR, trainers and managers retain only the business/\allowbreak{}content permissions approved by IT for their tasks. They are not system administrators. Vendor configuration/\allowbreak{}test access is limited to IT-approved sandbox accounts. Meridian IT controls any supervised production changes. WBS 3.1.1 configures SSO. 6.1.2 tests role access/\allowbreak{}security and 6.2.1 verifies latency.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 22
\item \textbf{Centralized training library:} Employees can open assigned digital training materials. Authorized instructors and administrators can upload, organize, and update approved content.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 23
\item \textbf{Role and department-based assignments:} Mandatory courses are assigned using employee role and department information and business rules approved by the compliance officer, Learning and Development team, and HR. Authorized managers and administrators can review and adjust assignments.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 24
\item \textbf{Online course completion:} Employees can complete eligible online courses. The LMS records progress and completion and makes those records available to authorized users.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 25
\item \textbf{Training and certification records:} Employee profiles retain training history, certification types, completion dates, and expiration dates where applicable. Authorized administrators can record verified certifications earned through external providers.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 26
\item \textbf{Compliance reporting:} Authorized managers, HR, and Compliance can generate standard reports showing course completion, overdue training, and upcoming certification expirations, filtered by employee, role, or department.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 27
\item \textbf{Administrative record maintenance:} Authorized administrators can correct employee information and training records through a documented process, with changes traceable to the person making them.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 28
\item \textbf{Operational monitoring:} Authorized IT and support staff can review sign-in latency, concurrent usage, availability, completion-recording delays and recovery evidence using dashboards, with indicators for breaches of the agreed targets.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Quality standards}\label{sec:1.5}
The LMS and digitized training materials will meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA accessibility requirements, Meridian's IT security and role-based access policies, and applicable employee-record privacy and retention requirements confirmed by HR and Compliance. Converted materials must preserve approved training content, and migrated records must meet the accuracy requirements in Section 1.4. Supporting documentation will follow consistent naming, versioning, and approval conventions. Section 6 defines the reviews, tests, and acceptance criteria used to verify these standards.

\subsection{Explicit exclusions}\label{sec:1.7}

Importing verified externally earned certification records under custom course certificates managed by the L\&D team in coordination with HR remains in scope.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 43
\item \textbf{Automated email and calendar notifications:} Integrating training reminders, certification-expiration alerts, or calendar invitations with Meridian's email and calendar systems. HR will review LMS reports and coordinate follow-up with department managers.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 44
\item \textbf{Off-network access:} Enabling LMS access from outside Meridian's internal company network. Departments will arrange access to approved network-connected devices and time for staff to complete training.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 45
\item \textbf{New curriculum development:} Creating new courses, instructional videos, or interactive learning materials beyond converting existing HR-approved content.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 46
\item \textbf{Hands-on clinical training:} BLS/\allowbreak{}ACLS practical instruction and skills assessments remain offline. Eligible digital materials and verified certification records remain supported.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 47
\item \textbf{Ongoing operations and future enhancements:} Operating the LMS or implementing additional features after formal project handover. Operational responsibilities will transfer to Meridian's designated teams, and deferred enhancements will be recorded in the Future Feature Register.

\subsection{Scope control process}\label{sec:1.10}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Verify:} Deliverable owners submit completed work against the dictionary's acceptance criteria. The designated stakeholder records acceptance, required corrections or rejection, with the approver's name, date and supporting evidence.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 57
\item \textbf{Monitor:} The project manager compares planned, active and completed work with the approved WBS each week. The requirements and verification record is updated as evidence is produced. Gaps, unapproved additions and changes to exclusions are flagged.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 58
\item \textbf{Prevent scope creep:} Requested work beyond the established baseline is logged as a change request before implementation. Defects are corrected against existing acceptance criteria. Additional functionality, new deliverables or changes to agreed criteria follow Section 1.11. Deferred enhancements are recorded in the Future Feature Register.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 59
\item \textbf{Control:} Approved changes are reflected in the scope statement, WBS, dictionary, schedule, budget and affected plans before implementation. Stakeholders receive the decision and current version references. Each phase proceeds only after its required acceptance and approval conditions are met.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Change request procedure}\label{sec:1.11}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Submit:} Anyone may submit a change request using the project's standard form, describing the proposed change, reason, expected benefit, and urgency.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 62
\item \textbf{Log:} The project manager assigns a change request ID and records it in the change log within one working day.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 63
\item \textbf{Analyze:} Within three working days, the project manager and affected work package owners assess impacts on scope, schedule, critical path, effort, budget, resources, risk, and quality.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 64
\item \textbf{Decide:} The appropriate authority approves, rejects, or defers the request within five working days of submission, using the approval limits below. The decision and rationale are recorded in the change log.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 65
\item \textbf{Update and communicate:} Before implementation, the project manager updates affected requirements, scope, WBS, dictionary, schedule, budget, and supporting plans. Revised baseline documents receive updated version numbers and are saved in the shared project folder. Affected stakeholders are notified.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 66
\item \textbf{Implement and close:} The assigned owner implements the approved change. The relevant stakeholder verifies the result against the updated acceptance criteria, and the project manager closes the request.
\end{enumerate}
