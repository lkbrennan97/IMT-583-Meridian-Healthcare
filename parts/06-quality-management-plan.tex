\subsubsection{Technical Standards}\label{sec:technical-standards}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Interoperability \& Content Standards:} The LMS must support vendor-compatible formats for existing HR-approved training materials, including PDF, DOCX, PPTX, and Markdown. Existing packaged courses must comply with SCORM 1.2 or xAPI standards where applicable and supported by the selected LMS for tracking learner progress, quiz scores, and course completion. Individual converted documents are not required to be converted into SCORM or xAPI packages. Any additional content development or packaging beyond the existing approved materials and courses is subject to the formal change request process defined in Section 1.11.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 226
\item \textbf{Security \& Privacy Standards:} Meridian IT owns RBAC, privileged administration, identity configuration and the IT-based LMS Administrator role. IT approves least-privilege business/\allowbreak{}content permissions for employees, HR, trainers and managers. The platform retains the specified HIPAA/\allowbreak{}PHI controls and requires Meridian InfoSec acceptance before go-live. Employee learner access remains internal-network only. IT may approve time-limited vendor sandbox/\allowbreak{}test accounts for implementation. These do not grant external learner access or unrestricted production access. Production changes use IT-controlled, supervised implementation procedures with audit logging.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 227
\item \textbf{Performance \& Availability Standards:} Provide at least 99\% monthly availability using maintenance exclusions defined in the vendor contract. At least 95\% of SSO sign-ins complete in under 3.0 seconds, measured from valid-credential submission to authenticated-session availability on Meridian's network. Page-load latency remains under 2.0 seconds at the 95th percentile under the representative 5,000-concurrent-user workload. Completed training appears in employee records and standard reports within 5 minutes. Recovery evidence supports service restoration within 8 hours and maximum recorded-activity loss of 24 hours. Shift-level observations supplement the monthly availability measure.
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}

Meridian OneDrive is the proposed repository for approved project and operational records, structured into a maximum of four directory levels. The PM publishes approved snapshots and maintains the version register. IT verifies retention settings and document-recovery arrangements before operational handover.

\item \textbf{Standard Naming Convention:} All project artifacts must adhere strictly to the standardized naming convention: MeridianLMS\_\allowbreak{}[TopFolder]\_\allowbreak{}[SecondFolder]\_\allowbreak{}[ThirdFolder]\_\allowbreak{}[ItemType]\_\allowbreak{}[Name\_\allowbreak{}YYYYMMDD].
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 232
\item \textbf{Version Control \& Governance:} Draft deliverables use v0.x. Approved baselines are retained as v1.0 PDF snapshots with their editable sources and approval records. The PM preserves superseded versions and increments approved revisions, for example v1.1, through change control. OneDrive version history supports document recovery subject to IT verification. No configured nightly automated backup job is assumed. LMS recovery requirements remain separate.
% Source GP4 Skeleton block 233
\item \textbf{Access Control:} Project-document permissions are separate from LMS permissions. PM maintains the approved document set. IT, HRIS and Compliance review it. Vendor leads receive only scoped technical-folder permissions. These document permissions confer no LMS learner or privileged production access. Meridian IT approves and audits sandbox implementation accounts separately.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Corrective action process}\label{sec:corrective-action-process}
When a quality metric misses its target or a defect is reported, the responsible owner records the evidence, affected WBS ID, severity, corrective action and due date in Jira. The Technical /\allowbreak{} Quality Lead coordinates cause analysis and retesting with the relevant delivery owner. Sev-1 incidents follow the immediate escalation and eight-elapsed-hour verified-fix rule. Release-blocking defects remain open until the required evidence is accepted.


% Source GP4 Skeleton block 262
The PM reports corrective-action status through Section 5.2. Actions that change scope, schedule, funding or resource commitments follow Section 1.11 before implementation. The assigned reviewer verifies the correction and retains the test result, decision and closure record with the deliverable evidence.
